## Situation

The FAA issued Emergency AD 2026-09-51 on 11 May 2026 affecting CFM56-5B engines with stage-2 high-pressure-turbine blade part number 338-101-407-0 installed. The AD requires either (a) immediate borescope inspection within the next 75 flight cycles or (b) replacement of the affected blades.

Our fleet has 18 aircraft with affected engines. The cyclic compliance window is tight: based on current average daily utilization, approximately 6 aircraft must be inspected within 7 calendar days, and the remainder within 21 calendar days.

EASA has not yet adopted an equivalent AD, but is expected to within 48 hours. The European Commission has been notified.
