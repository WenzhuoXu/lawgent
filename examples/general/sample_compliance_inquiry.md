# Compliance Inquiry — Cross-Border Data Transfer

**From:** Product Counsel, Beijing Lingdun Technology Co., Ltd.
**To:** Legal Helper (compliance-check)
**Date:** 2026-05-10

## Action / Initiative

We plan to launch a new SaaS analytics product (the "Insight Platform")
that processes purchase-history data of our PRC retail customers and
transmits aggregated metrics to a US-based analytics partner ("Partner")
for benchmarking. Personal information of approximately 2 million PRC
data subjects is involved.

## Entity

Beijing Lingdun Technology Co., Ltd. (主体: 北京灵盾科技有限公司),
licensed in Beijing.

## Geographic scope

Data subjects: PRC. Storage: primary in Beijing; replicated to AWS
us-east-1 for analytics. Partner: Delaware-incorporated, processes data
in US only.

## Timeline

Soft launch 2026-08-01.

## Data involved

- Customer name, mobile number (hashed at rest), purchase history,
  product preferences, location to city level.
- ~5% of records include sensitive personal information (financial
  account numbers required for refunds).

## Question

What PRC and US legal requirements apply, what cross-border transfer
pathway is appropriate, and which approvals / filings / contracts must be
in place before launch? Identify reporting duties to the CAC, MIIT, and
any other authority. Flag risk areas and suggest next steps.
