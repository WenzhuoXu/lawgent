# Employment Contract (劳动合同)

**Effective:** 2026-06-01
**Parties:** Beijing Lingdun Technology Co., Ltd. ("Employer") and Zhang San ("Employee").

## 1. Position
Employee is engaged as Senior Software Engineer reporting to the CTO of
the Employer's Beijing office.

## 2. Term
Fixed-term: **three (3) years** from the Effective Date, automatically
renewable absent written notice from either party at least 30 days before
expiry.

## 3. Probation
Probation period: **six (6) months** from the Effective Date.

## 4. Compensation
Base salary: RMB 45,000 per month, payable on the 15th of each following
month. Annual performance bonus up to 3 months of base salary subject to
individual + company performance targets.

## 5. Working Hours
Standard 40-hour work week (5 × 8 hours). Overtime per applicable Labour
Law and Employer's overtime policy. Annual leave: 15 working days after
1 year of service, in accordance with PRC regulations.

## 6. Confidentiality
Employee shall not disclose Employer's trade secrets, customer lists,
source code, or business plans during employment or for **three (3)
years** thereafter. This obligation does not bar disclosure required by
court order or regulator inquiry.

## 7. Intellectual Property
All work product created within the scope of employment or using
Employer's resources is service invention / 职务作品 owned by Employer
under PRC Patent Law and PRC Copyright Law. Employee shall promptly
disclose and assist with registration.

## 8. Non-Competition
After termination, Employee shall not work for a direct competitor in
the PRC for **twelve (12) months**, in consideration of monthly
compensation equal to **30% of monthly base salary** during the
restricted period. Geographic scope: PRC.

## 9. Termination
- By mutual agreement; or
- Either party with 30 days' written notice (Employee) / per PRC Labour
  Contract Law (Employer); or
- Immediate termination for cause as defined in Article 39 of the Labour
  Contract Law.

Severance per PRC Labour Contract Law applies where required by statute.

## 10. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution
PRC Labour Law and Labour Contract Law govern. Disputes go first to
mediation, then to Beijing Labour Dispute Arbitration Commission, then to
the People's Court if either party is dissatisfied with the arbitral
award.
