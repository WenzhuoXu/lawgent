# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

**Effective Date:** 2026-04-15
**Parties:**
- Beijing Lingdun Technology Co., Ltd. ("Discloser/Receiver A"), incorporated in the PRC.
- Acme Cloud, Inc. ("Discloser/Receiver B"), incorporated in Delaware, USA.

## 1. Purpose
The parties wish to explore a potential commercial relationship involving the
integration of A's data-platform services with B's cloud infrastructure
(the "Purpose"). To facilitate discussions, each party may disclose to the
other certain non-public technical, business, or financial information
("Confidential Information").

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by one party
("Discloser") to the other ("Receiver"), in any form, that is marked or
identified as confidential at disclosure, or that a reasonable person would
understand to be confidential under the circumstances.

## 3. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that:
(a) is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement;
(b) was rightfully in Receiver's possession before disclosure;
(c) is independently developed without use of the Confidential Information;
(d) is rightfully received from a third party without a confidentiality duty.

## 4. Obligations
Each Receiver shall:
(a) use Confidential Information solely for the Purpose;
(b) protect it with the same care it uses for its own confidential
information, but no less than a reasonable standard;
(c) restrict access to employees and contractors with a need to know who
are bound by written confidentiality obligations at least as protective.

## 5. Term and Survival
This Agreement is effective for **two (2) years** from the Effective Date.
Confidentiality obligations survive for **five (5) years** thereafter.

## 6. Permitted Disclosures
Receiver may disclose Confidential Information:
(a) as required by applicable law, regulation, or court order — Receiver
shall give Discloser prompt written notice where lawful;
(b) to professional advisers under duty of confidentiality.

## 7. No Residual Rights
This Agreement does not grant any licence, ownership, or other right in
Confidential Information beyond what is necessary for the Purpose. No
residuals clause applies.

## 8. Personal Information
If Confidential Information includes personal information of PRC data
subjects, Receiver shall process such information in accordance with the
PRC Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL) and shall cooperate with
Discloser on cross-border transfer pathways (CAC SCC or 安全评估).

## 9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China.
Any dispute shall be submitted to the China International Economic and
Trade Arbitration Commission (CIETAC) in Beijing under the rules in
effect at the time of arbitration.

## 10. No Obligation to Proceed
Nothing in this Agreement obligates either party to enter into any further
agreement.
