# Aviation overlay — /brief

Layered on top of `skills/brief/SKILL.md` when the aviation domain pack is
active. Add the following aviation-specific scope to every brief mode.

## Additional sources to scan (Daily)

- **Email / chat**: FAA Letters of Investigation, EASA findings, NTSB
  requests, AD / SB notifications, SAFOs, ramp-incident escalations,
  counterparty responses on lessor / lessee / OEM / financier negotiations.
- **Calendar**: lease closings, MRO RFP reviews, regulator meetings, AOC
  inspections, AD compliance deadlines, IR filings, redelivery windows,
  AOC renewals, insurance renewals, industry events (NBAA / IATA / FSF).
- **CLM**: aircraft sale/lease, charter, MRO SOWs, IGTAs awaiting review;
  redelivery within 90 days; expiring IGTA / MRO agreements.
- **CRM / fleet**: aircraft transitions (deliveries, redeliveries, total
  losses); new routes / station openings for legal review.
- **Safety / maintenance**: open SDRs / occurrence reports flagged for
  legal evaluation; AD compliance status flagged red.

## Topic-mode regulatory scope

Add to the authority hierarchy:

- International conventions: Chicago Convention; ICAO Annexes 1–19 and
  SARPs (esp. Annex 6 ops, Annex 8 airworthiness, Annex 9 facilitation,
  Annex 13 accident investigation, Annex 17 security); Tokyo Convention
  (1963) + Montreal Protocol 2014 (MP14); Warsaw / Montreal Convention
  (passenger liability); Cape Town Convention + Aircraft Protocol.
- National: 14 CFR (FAA), 49 CFR (DOT/TSA/NTSB/PHMSA), EASA Implementing
  Rules + AMC/GM, CCAR (CAAC), JCAB rules, DGCA rules.
- Export control: ITAR (22 CFR 120-130), EAR (15 CFR 730-774), BIS Entity
  List, DDTC Commodity Jurisdiction.

For onboard-passenger incidents, surface Findings bullets covering: factual
trigger; crew/captain immediate-action authority; notification (OCC,
destination authorities, security handoff); preservation (CVR / FDR / QAR /
EFB / CCTV / PNR / API / manifest, post-landing handover); limits (no crew
immigration adjudication, restraint / search / seizure limits absent
safety + captain authority + local law). See aviation playbook §0 for the
required label set.

## Incident-mode aviation deadlines

| Authority | Trigger | Window |
|---|---|---|
| NTSB | 14 CFR § 830.5 | Immediate ("by the most expeditious means available") |
| FAA SDR | 14 CFR § 121.703 / § 135.415 | 96 hours |
| State of registry | ICAO Annex 13 | As soon as reasonably practicable |
| State of operator | ICAO Annex 13 | ASAP |
| EU Reg. 376 | Occurrence | 72 hours |
| Family assistance | 49 USC § 1136 / EU Reg. 996/2010 | Activate per plan |
| CAAC | CCAR-395 / 民用航空器事件调查规定 | Per applicable rule |

Preservation list: CVR / FDR / QAR / EFB / dispatch / weight & balance /
fueling / maintenance / training records. Privilege: investigations under
counsel direction; Upjohn warnings to interviewed employees.

Key contacts: Accountable Manager / DOO / DOS / DOM; insurance broker +
claims contact; outside aviation counsel (emergency-response); crisis
communications; state-of-occurrence / registry / operator regulator points
of contact.
