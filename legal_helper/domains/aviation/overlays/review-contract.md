# Aviation overlay — /review-contract

Layered when the aviation domain pack is active. Adds aviation-specific
contract types and clause checks.

## Aviation contract types

- **Aircraft sale & purchase** — APA / SPA, escrow / delivery, lien
  searches, IR sale + discharge filings, bilateral export airworthiness.
- **Operating lease / finance lease / sale-leaseback** — single-investor
  vs JOLCO / JOL / EETC; cross-border tax structures.
- **Wet lease / ACMI / charter** — AOC mismatch checks, cabotage,
  substituted aircraft equivalence, insurance writebacks.
- **MRO / Part-145 services** — heavy / line / component / engine; SLA;
  AOG response; capability list scope.
- **OEM** — purchase agreement, warranty + product liability, parts pool,
  upgrade roadmap, training credits.
- **IGTA** — inter-airline ground / technical assistance; mutual
  service-credit baskets.
- **Pool / Loan** — parts pool, engine pool, exchange / loan agreements.
- **Charter customer / broker** — ACMI, executive charter, MOU, master
  services.

## Aviation-specific clauses to check (per playbook §1–§13)

- **Hull & liability insurance** — minimums per playbook §1; AVN52E /
  AVN67B / LSW555D endorsements; severability of interest; waiver of
  subrogation; 30/7-day cancellation notice.
- **Cape Town / IDERA** — IDERA in approved form, DOR / DOR-EQ, IR
  filings, Article 13 declaration check (per playbook §2).
- **Section 1110 protection** — no waiver, no disguised-loan language
  (playbook §3).
- **AD / SB allocation** — pre-existing vs post-delivery, $1M default
  threshold, mandatory vs alert vs desirable (playbook §4).
- **Return conditions** — hours / cycles to next major check, LLP minimum
  remaining cycles, EGT margin, records continuity, EOL compensation,
  acceptance tests (playbook §5).
- **Limitation of liability** — hell-or-high-water rent; broad lessee
  indemnity; lessor liability limited to gross negligence + wilful
  misconduct; MRO cap 2× work-order value (playbook §6).
- **Confidentiality** — mutual default; 2–3y term, 5y survival; regulator
  / safety carveouts (CAAC / FAA / EASA / NTSB / AAIB / BEA / BFU / ICAO);
  no residuals clause; ITAR / EAR acknowledgment (playbook §7).
- **Governing law** — NY law / English law preferred; Irish for leasing;
  Singapore / HK for Asia-Pac; sovereign-immunity waiver for state-owned
  operators (playbook §8).
- **Assignment / novation** — rated lessor list, IDERA reissuance + IR
  re-filing + insurance + regulator non-objection (playbook §9).
- **Sub-lease / wet-lease** — lessor consent (not unreasonably withheld);
  AOC approval; ITAR clearance; state-of-registry change (playbook §10).
- **Export control** — ITAR / EAR / OFAC posture; deemed-export risk;
  denied-party screening (playbook §11).
- **Charter / ACMI** — ACMI insurance posture, substitution rights,
  cancellation fees, ground-stop writebacks (playbook §12).

## Aviation cross-jurisdictional traps

- Cape Town effectiveness depends on Article 13 declarations of the state
  of registry; verify.
- AOC scope vs operating territory; cabotage rules; foreign-carrier
  permits.
- Tax characterisation (true lease vs disguised loan) drives Section 1110
  and §168 depreciation.
- Charter ACMI under Part 121 vs Part 135 vs EASA NCC; engine-leasing
  cross-border depreciation interplay.
