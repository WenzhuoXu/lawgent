# Aviation overlay — /legal-risk-assessment

Layered when the aviation domain pack is active.

## Additional risk categories

- **Airworthiness** — AD / SB non-compliance, TC/STC scope breach, design
  change without DER / ODA support, MEL deviation.
- **Certificate / approval** — Part-145 / Part-CAMO / AOC scope creep,
  OpSpec breach, LOA misuse, repair-station capability mismatch.
- **Accident / incident** — NTSB / ICAO Annex 13 reporting failures, CVR /
  FDR / QAR / EFB preservation failures, late insurer / lessor / family-
  assistance notification.
- **Cape Town / IDERA** — defective IDERA, IR filing gaps, state-of-registry
  declaration mismatch, novation without IDERA reissuance.
- **Charter / ACMI** — substituted aircraft outside scope, AOC mismatch,
  cabotage breach, hostile-environment routing without insurance writeback.
- **FAR 117 / EU FTL / CCAR-121-R7** — flight & duty exposure, fatigue
  reporting, in-flight rest-facility class.
- **Export control (aviation)** — ITAR USML Cat. VIII (military aircraft) /
  EAR ECCN 9A6xx / 9A9xx / 9E6xx (deemed exports, foreign-national MRO
  personnel, country of ultimate use).
- **FOQA / ASAP data** — disclosure outside Order 8000.82 / EU Reg. 376
  carveouts; enforcement-use exposure.
- **Environmental** — CORSIA non-compliance, EU ETS Aviation surrender
  failure, ICAO Annex 16 Vol I/II noise + emissions breach.
- **Passenger-handling** — Tokyo / MP14 misuse of restraint, document
  destruction handoff, EU 261 / DOT delay-compensation failure.

## Aviation-specific escalation triggers (add to general triggers)

- Any RED band on airworthiness, certificate, accident, or Cape Town.
- Any IDERA defect identified mid-deal.
- Any ITAR / EAR controlled-data exposure.
- Any mandatory regulator-disclosure restriction inside an NDA.
- Any safety-data disclosure outside ASAP MOU.
